# Course Card — Guided Dialogue

Identifier: course_card

## A. Learning goal

A well-supported justification contains a conclusion, two relevant pieces of evidence, and one limitation.

## B. Teaching support

When evidence is missing, guided dialogue brings the learner back to the material and asks a focused question. Receiving a ready-made conclusion alone does not demonstrate understanding.

## C. Limitation

Individual dialogue takes time. After an initial attempt and revision, an instructor may provide an explained example for comparison.
